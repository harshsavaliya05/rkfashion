Returns & Exchanges Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

Please read our return and exchange guidelines carefully before placing an order on RK Fashion.

7-Day Size Exchange Only

We accept size exchange requests within 7 days of delivery. Please note: we offer **Exchanges Only**, and no monetary refunds are issued unless the item is completely out of stock.

Conditions for Exchange

To qualify for an exchange, your item must meet the following criteria:

  • The item must be completely unworn, unwashed, and undamaged.
  • All original brand tags, pricing labels, and barcode tags must remain attached to the garment.
  • The product must be returned in its original product packaging.

How to Initiate an Exchange

Please follow these steps to request a size exchange:

  1. Email our customer support team at support@rkfashion.com with your Order ID and the new size requested.
  2. We will schedule a reverse pickup from your address within 48 hours.
  3. Once we receive the returned item and inspect its condition, we will ship the replacement size to you free of cost.
